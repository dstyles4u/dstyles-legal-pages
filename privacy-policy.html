1. Who Is Responsible for Your Information

D’Styles VIP Barber, operated by Kevin Smith in Hiram, Georgia, is responsible for the personal information described in this Privacy Policy, except where an independent third-party provider processes information under its own terms and privacy practices.

2. Operational Definitions

Personal Information: Information that identifies, relates to, describes, or may reasonably be associated with an individual, subject to applicable law.

Transactional Communication: A message necessary to administer an appointment, membership, payment, purchase, account, security matter, or requested service.

Marketing Communication: A promotional or nonessential commercial message concerning services, memberships, products, publications, announcements, or offers.

Subscriber: A person who voluntarily enrolls to receive The Hiram Report or another approved publication communication.

Member: An approved adult client enrolled in an active D’Styles membership program. A minor participating through an approved Kids Membership does not hold independent membership status.

Automated Workflow: A technology-supported process used to send messages, update records, monitor events, or support administration.

Service Provider: An external provider supporting scheduling, payments, communications, analytics, fulfillment, hosting, membership administration, or related operations.

3. Information That May Be Collected

Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Appointment details and service selections
  • Membership requests and account information
  • Messages, forms, inquiries, purchases, delivery information, and communication preferences
  • Allergies, sensitivities, or service concerns when voluntarily disclosed

Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser and device type
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Pages viewed, referral source, date, time, and interaction data
  • Cookie, performance, and similar identifier information

Information Received from Providers

  • Booking, membership, payment, delivery, and subscriber status
  • Email or text delivery status
  • Fraud, security, and fulfillment signals

Payment-card information may be submitted directly to a third-party processor. D’Styles may receive limited transaction details such as payment status, amount, billing date, payment-method type, and limited account identifiers.

4. How Information May Be Used

  • Schedule appointments and deliver services
  • Administer VIP and approved Kids Membership add-ons
  • Process payments and failed-payment recovery
  • Send confirmations, reminders, notices, and status updates
  • Deliver The Hiram Report and approved subscriber communications
  • Process orders, digital access, shipping, and fulfillment
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security concerns
  • Maintain consent, opt-out, tax, and transaction records
  • Measure performance and improve workflows
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce agreements

5. Appointment Information

Appointment information may be used to schedule services, manage availability, communicate details, administer cancellation and no-show policies, maintain service history, resolve balances, and protect booking eligibility.

6. VIP Membership Information

Membership information may be used to evaluate requests, manage recurring billing, administer benefits, monitor eligibility, control booking access, send failed-payment notices, process cancellations, manage suspension, and review reapplication.

Failed recurring-membership payments follow a defined recovery process: notification, up to three retry attempts, a three-day grace period, and suspension of membership benefits if the payment remains unresolved.

VIP Membership approval is not automated. Final decisions remain under Kevin Smith’s control and are reviewed according to fit and current capacity.

7. Kids Membership and Household Rules

Kids Memberships may be available only as an approved add-on to a qualifying parent or legal guardian membership. They are not publicly advertised and do not create independent membership status, account ownership, or contractual authority for the minor.

The parent or legal guardian remains responsible for scheduling, payments, communications, cancellations, service decisions, and policy compliance.

A parent membership does not automatically extend benefits to every family or household member. Kids Membership eligibility is determined by the active parent membership structure and current capacity.

Membership benefits are personal and non-transferable unless specifically authorized in writing.

8. The Hiram Report and Knowledge Center

Subscribers may provide contact information to receive issue notices, newsletters, event announcements, and related publication communications.

Subscription creates publication-subscriber status only. It does not create VIP Membership, appointment priority, or another paid status.

Knowledge Center access generally does not require personal information unless a visitor voluntarily submits a form, subscribes, or interacts with website analytics.

9. Store, Purchases, and Digital Commerce

Product purchases, digital offerings, memberships, and future store transactions may require information necessary to process orders, prevent fraud, deliver access, fulfill purchases, manage shipping, and maintain transaction records.

10. Transactional and Marketing Communications

Transactional Communications

D’Styles may send appointment confirmations, reminders, cancellation notices, billing updates, failed-payment notices, membership updates, purchase confirmations, delivery notices, and security alerts.

Certain communications may be necessary to administer an active relationship. Opting out may affect the ability to provide or maintain the related service.

Marketing Communications

Marketing emails and texts may include promotions, announcements, membership education, publication updates, or store campaigns. They may be declined through the available opt-out method.

Marketing SMS consent should be collected separately at enrollment. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help when those instructions apply to the active program.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

11. Automation Transparency

Automated workflows may process confirmations, reminders, failed-payment notices, membership updates, subscriber communications, purchase notifications, and operational alerts.

Automation assists administration. It does not guarantee appointment availability, VIP Membership approval, reinstatement, consideration-list acceptance, or immediate response times.

Final VIP Membership decisions remain under Kevin Smith’s control.

12. Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

  • Essential cookies for core site operation
  • Security cookies for sessions, forms, and transactions
  • Preference cookies for selected settings
  • Analytics cookies for usage and performance
  • Conversion measurement for bookings, subscriptions, and purchases

Browser settings may allow users to block or remove certain cookies. Disabling cookies may affect functionality.

The website may support a consent banner or preference-management system as technology, legal requirements, and tracking practices evolve.

13. How Information May Be Disclosed

  • Scheduling providers
  • Payment processors
  • Communication providers
  • Membership systems
  • Fulfillment partners
  • Analytics and security providers
  • Professional advisers
  • Legal authorities when required

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, enforce agreements, or respond to lawful requests.

14. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, administer appointments and memberships, process transactions, maintain consent and opt-out records, meet tax and legal obligations, investigate fraud, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and support legitimate operations.

Information may be deleted, anonymized, archived, or securely disposed of when no longer reasonably required, subject to legal and operational obligations.

Internal governance should cover appointment, membership, transaction, consent, SMS opt-out, email opt-out, subscriber, security-log, and inquiry records.

15. Privacy Choices and Request Process

Requests may be submitted directly to Kevin at kevin@dstyles4u.com.

When applicable, requests may concern access, correction, marketing opt-out, communication preferences, deletion, consent withdrawal, or information about use or disclosure.

Reasonable identity verification may be required before information is disclosed, corrected, or deleted. Response timing depends on request complexity, workload, system access, and applicable law.

Certain records may be retained for appointments, memberships, payments, taxes, consent history, fraud prevention, security, disputes, legal compliance, or enforcement of agreements.

16. Children and Minors

The website and online services are intended for adults. Information relating to minors must be submitted and managed by a parent or legal guardian.

D’Styles does not knowingly seek to collect membership, communication, account, or publication-subscription information directly from children without appropriate parental involvement.

No direct child accounts, child newsletter subscriptions, child membership applications, child-controlled billing profiles, or child-controlled communication preferences are permitted.

17. Security and Incident Response

D’Styles uses reasonable administrative, technical, and operational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Suspected unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure may be investigated. Notifications may be provided when required by applicable law or reasonably appropriate.

Security reviews may result in temporary restrictions on website, booking, payment, account, or membership access while an incident is evaluated.

18. External Links and Third-Party Services

External websites operate under their own terms and privacy practices. D’Styles is not responsible for the independent privacy, security, content, or data-handling practices of third parties.

19. Policy Updates and Website Governance

Changes become effective on the stated effective date. Additional notice or consent will be provided where required.

The Policy should be reviewed when software or communication channels are added, membership terms change, store operations launch, automation changes materially, or data-collection practices change.

Each material review should update the Effective Date, Last Updated Date, Consent Version Number, internal vendor register, public disclosures, consent forms, automation logic, and retention rules.
